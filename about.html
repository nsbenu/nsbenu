About

Who we are

NSBE at Northwestern is the campus chapter of the National Society of Black Engineers, the largest student-governed organization in the country dedicated to Black engineering students.

01

Our mission

We bring together undergraduate and graduate students across McCormick's departments — from computer science to biomedical, mechanical, and civil engineering — for mentorship, professional development, and community. Our chapter was founded in [year] and has grown to [N] active members.

Our work rests on NSBE's three pillars, and we build our programming around them.

  • Academic ExcellenceStudy halls, tutoring networks, and peer mentorship across core McCormick sequences.
  • Professional DevelopmentResume workshops, mock interviews, and direct lines to recruiters at partner companies.
  • Community ServiceK-12 STEM outreach in the Evanston and Chicago area, introducing younger students to engineering.

Join the chapter

Membership is open to all Northwestern students — you don't need to be an engineering major to get involved. National dues are set by NSBE; chapter dues (if any) help fund our events.

  1. Create an account at nsbe.org and register for national membership.
  2. Fill out the Northwestern chapter interest form (link below).
  3. Come to a General Body Meeting — no commitment needed to check us out.
